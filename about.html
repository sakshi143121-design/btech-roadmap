Read this when you feel stuck

Everyone starts at zero.Nobody was born knowing loops or arrays. The students you admire were beginners once, and they kept going.
Small steps win.One hour a day for a year is more than 350 hours. You do not need a perfect day, just a regular one.
Errors are teachers.Every red error message is a hint. Read it slowly, change one thing, and try again. That is real programming.
Race only yourself.Someone will always be ahead of you. Ask if you are better than last month. If yes, you are winning.

Follow these 5 steps in order. Tap a step to open it.

1Understand B.TechKnow the course and your branch 2Pick a languageC, C++, Python, Java or JavaScript 3Follow your yearGoals, routine and a readiness check 4Practice dailyNotes, videos and the topic tracker 5Build and applyProjects, internships, interviews

A simple 90-minute daily routine

30 minLearn: one video or one topic from the notes
30 minPractice: solve 2 or 3 problems yourself
15 minRevise: write what you learned in your own words
15 minBuild: add one small thing to your project
Your first 7 days, if you are a complete beginner
  1. Day 1: install VS Code and make your first page.
  2. Day 2: write a "Hello, World" program in one language.
  3. Day 3: learn variables and take input from the user.
  4. Day 4: learn if-else and loops.
  5. Day 5: solve 3 easy problems without looking at the answer.
  6. Day 6: make a small web page about yourself.
  7. Day 7: upload your work to GitHub and be proud of it.
